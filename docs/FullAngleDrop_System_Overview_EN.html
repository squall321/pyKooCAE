⚙② Build the Model

KooMeshModifier turns each direction into one solver-ready .k — it rotates the model and attaches the floor and conditions.

  • 1
    Rotate the product to the target pose — roll · pitch · yaw
  • 2
    Create the drop floor — plane / graded mesh / rigid wall
  • 3
    Define contact — self-contact (SINGLE_SURFACE), excluding bonded faces
  • 4
    Solver controls & initial velocity — timestep stabilization, drop height v=√(2gh)
  • 5
    Boundary & output — floor fixed / stress-wave absorption, d3plot · contact force

▦③ Aggregate Results

Just one idea — deep is one run, sphere aggregates by direction, impact aggregates by position.

deepsingle
Reads one analysis

Pulls per-part peak stress, strain and safety factor from the d3plot into result.json, then deletes the d3plot.

spheredirection
Paints every direction on a sphere

All directions' deep results as a spherical heatmap → see at a glance which orientation is most dangerous.

impactposition
Paints every position on a face

Results across many impact positions on a struck face, shown as a distribution → which position is most dangerous.

On one fixed lattice, full analysis and adaptive sampling differ only in run order — adaptive simply finds the dangerous directions first.
